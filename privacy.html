Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Clearpath Accounting (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over your information.

1. Who we are

Clearpath Accounting provides accounting, tax, bookkeeping and business advisory services to sole traders, freelancers and small businesses.

For privacy enquiries, contact us at hello@clearpathaccounting.co.uk or call 01234 567 890.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or use our services:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and business name.
  • Details of your enquiry, including the services you are interested in.
  • Accounting and tax records you provide to us, such as income, expenses, invoices, bank statements and tax documents.
  • Communication records, including emails, telephone notes and WhatsApp messages.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, where analytics tools are used.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide accounting services.
  • Prepare accounts, tax returns, VAT returns and related documentation.
  • Meet legal, regulatory and anti-money-laundering obligations.
  • Send service updates, invoices and important deadline reminders.
  • Improve our website and services.

4. Legal bases for processing

We process personal information where it is necessary to perform a contract with you, comply with a legal obligation, pursue our legitimate business interests, or where we have your consent.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers, such as cloud hosting, email, accounting software and IT support providers, only where necessary to deliver our services.

We may also share information where required by law, regulation, court order, or with HMRC and other relevant authorities.

6. WhatsApp communications

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages and contact number are processed by WhatsApp/Meta in accordance with their own privacy policy. We use WhatsApp messages to respond to your enquiry and provide customer support.

7. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, meet legal and regulatory requirements, and resolve disputes. Accounting and tax records are normally retained for the period required by applicable law.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to request a copy of your information in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact hello@clearpathaccounting.co.uk.

10. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, secure storage and staff confidentiality procedures. No online transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive documents through unsecured channels where possible.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, together with the date it was last updated.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact:
